# Your recording checklist

This is a preparation outline, not a script. The assignment requires you to speak in your own words, on camera, while demonstrating the app. Record at least six minutes. A screen recording with your face visible in a corner is suitable.

Before recording:

- Read one supplied transcript yourself and identify where the actual conversation differs from its logged outcome. Private reading notes are in the ignored `research/` folder locally.
- Rehearse starting a call, choosing results, recording the prospect's exact words, moving between stages, ending early, and finding the saved call.
- Use fictional sample notes for the Gemini demonstration. Never upload the transcript corpus or expose an API key, database password, hosting settings, or another private tab on camera.
- Wake the Render backend by opening the health URL, then confirm the app says the workspace is connected.

Topics to cover naturally (roughly 6–8 minutes, not memorised lines):

1. Who this screen is for and the problem it solves during a real cold call.
2. A walkthrough using your chosen supplied transcript: what the caller hears, why the chosen stage matters, and which quick choice fits. Capture the need exactly and explain a correction or objection.
3. Finish with an outcome that matches the conversation. Show where the call ended and that the original words survived in history.
4. Use synthetic notes to show the optional AI summary. Explain why you put AI after the call rather than in the live caller's way. Review its result against the original record.
5. One design decision supported by a particular transcript, one tradeoff, and what you left out. Explain these yourself.
6. Briefly show the public README and the healthy backend. Be ready to explain Vercel → Render → Supabase and that the keys stay on Render.

After recording:

- Play it back. Check your face is visible, the app is readable, audio is clear, and the duration is at least 6:00.
- Upload the video itself to Google Drive. Set General access to “Anyone with the link” and role to Viewer.
- Check the link opens without requesting access. Use the actual Drive video file link, not a folder, shortened URL, or another video provider.
- Provide that link in the chat so the assignment form can be completed with the verified repository.

The WhatsApp message and personal answers must be written by you, according to the task brief. The application page opens that step only after accepting the repo and video.
